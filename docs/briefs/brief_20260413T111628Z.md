**3) Risks & Limitations**
*   **Data Bias:** Insights are limited to Xiaohongshu’s user base (predominantly female, younger, urban, and digitally savvy). This may underrepresent key decision-makers like fathers or users from less tier-1 cities.
*   **Sample & Causality:** The data shows correlation, not causation. High discussion volume does not directly translate to willingness to pay for AI tools. The sample, while substantial for social listening, is not statistically projectable to the entire mainland DSE-aspirant population.
*   **Compliance & Sensitivity:** Topics like “identity” (身份) and cross-border system comparisons are inherently sensitive. Marketing AI tools in this space must navigate regulations on private tutoring, data privacy (PIPL), and educational advertising.
*   **Market Maturity:** Frequent mentions of existing schools and agencies suggest a crowded informational landscape. AI tools must clearly differentiate from free peer advice and established consultancy services.
